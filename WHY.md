# WHY
Accessibility: Non-technical staff can manage websites via intuitive interfaces, ensuring subject matter experts control the content strategy. 
Consistency and Branding: Pre-built templates and global change capabilities ensure uniform design and structure across all pages and channels. 
Collaboration: Built-in user roles, permissions, and workflows allow multiple team members to work simultaneously without conflicts. 
Scalability and Reach: A single CMS can power websites, mobile apps, and digital signage from one central repository, supporting business growth. 
SEO and Security: Most platforms offer built-in SEO tools to improve search visibility and robust security features to protect against threats.
